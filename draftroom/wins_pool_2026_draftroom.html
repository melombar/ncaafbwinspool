Overperform Targets — pre-draftable tailwinds & headwinds

This is a COUNT of team-specific reasons, not a prediction and not a draft order. Aggregate signals (SP+/TARP/RetProd) don't predict beating the number, so they're excluded — only structural + pod reasons you can see and overrule are counted. The market total stays the baseline; round, price, and scarcity still govern the pick. Hover a tag for the reason. Teams marked pod pending are schedule-only until their pod loads (Pac-12, Sun Belt & Mountain West loaded). The per-team tally is ordered by projected wins (the market line) so it reads as a pecking order — scan down to count how many teams sit in front of a target. Underperform targets (structural headwinds outweigh tailwinds) are flagged red ▼. A pod bet-against is shown separately as a POD FADE badge with a plain-language reason line — it's an override, independent of the net tally, so a team can be net-positive AND a pod fade. Where a pod cited a moved line, the O/U shows →X▲.

Upside Availability — urgency to land an upside team

Parallel to Anchor Availability. Count & color key on strong (+2 or better) upside teams still on the board per conference — those create real scarcity pressure. Cells show every +1-or-better team; soft (+1) ones are dimmed/italic so you don't lose them but can see where the strong leans sit. Color = your urgency: hot only for conferences you still need whose strong-upside supply is draining (filled and deep-refilling conferences stay cool). A thin strong-upside conference is also one rivals will target — so it doubles as a read on what others may grab.
