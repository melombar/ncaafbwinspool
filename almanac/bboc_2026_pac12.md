*Source: Big Bets on Campus 2026 Pac-12 preview (Stuckey / Collin Wilson / Duck). Roster = 2026 pool canon. Numbers: market = SBD/composite; SP+ = Connelly Mar-2026; Ret Prod = ESPN; Net TARP = Action.*

> **Flex-game flag:** real Pac-12 teams carry an 11-vs-12 game ambiguity (Week-13 flex game unsanctioned). Reassigned P4 teams play clean 12-game slates.
> **Unbalanced-schedule flag:** Pac-12 teams do NOT all play each other — the specific in-conference draw (who a team avoids vs draws) can swing its win total meaningfully vs the market. Captured per team in `Key Avoids/Draws` + Notes.

> **Note on Adj Conf:** unusually, *every* team in the 2026 Pac-12 bucket is adjusted (the conf is fully rebuilt from MWC / Pac-2 / reassigned P4). In most other conferences this column is blank for nearly all teams.
