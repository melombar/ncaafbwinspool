Confirm the game-count basis before treating any Pac-12 total as comparable to other conferences.

## Per-team BBOC notes (qualitative edge)

### Boise State — mkt 7.5 · Collin sees ~7 (lean under)
- **QB (priority):** Maddox Madsen returns — **flagged hard**: ~35 turnover-worthy plays, dreadful when tied/trailing (54% completion, 7 picks in those spots). Collin's read: over-reliant on the run game.
- **Coaching:** two new coordinators — Zach Hill (OC), Terrence Brown (DC).
- **Take:** conference anchor by the market, but Collin is below the number; the QB downgrade + coordinator turnover is the reason. Anchor-tier by SP+ (#39) but not a clean over.

### Colorado State — mkt 3.5 · Collin projects ~2.8 (hard under)
- **QB (priority):** new QB battle — Haas Haney (TCU→Oklahoma State transfer) vs. Darius Curry.
- **Coaching:** **new HC Jim Mora Jr.** (from UConn), **new OC Price Tracy**. Full offensive reset.
- **Take:** bottom of the bucket. New HC + new OC + unsettled QB = high variance, low floor. Collin well under the market.

### Fresno State — mkt 7.5 (BBOC) / 6.5 (board) · Collin ~4.5 (hard under)
- **QB (priority):** new QB Christian Martin (from Maryland), replacing EJ Warner.
- **Take:** **Collin very bearish, Duck bullish** — biggest host disagreement in the pod. SP+ (−2.3, #78) sides with the bearish read against the market. Treat as a fade candidate despite the market total.

### San Diego State — mkt 6.5
- **QB (priority):** Jayden Denegal — health-dependent (surgically repaired shoulder); deep-ball arm, struggles vs. cover 2/3.
- **Coaching:** Demetrius Sumler promoted to DC.
- **Take:** market/SP+ disagree sharply (mkt 6.5 vs SP+ #71). Highest ret prod (59%) in the bucket. QB health is the swing variable.

### Utah State — mkt 5.0 (BBOC) / 4.5 (board) · Collin under 5.5
- **QB (priority):** McKay Hillstead vs. Grady Brosterhous (battle).
- **Coaching:** **new HC Bronco Mendenhall**, **new OC Robert Anae** ("polarizing"), Nick Howell on defense.
- **Take:** full staff turnover; SP+ near the bottom of the bucket (−7.7, #97). Collin leans under.

## Draft read (per operating model)

Applying A1–A10 × S1–S5 (anchor value × conference depth) to this bucket:

- **Boise State is the lone clear anchor** (mkt 7.5, SP+ #39) — but the QB flag means it's not a strong *over*, just the best available top-line. It's the S1 of this conference.
- **The 6.5 tier is a market/SP+ battleground:** Oklahoma State (SP+ #38) and San Diego State (ret prod 59%) look more solid than Fresno State (Collin fade) and Texas State (SP+ #89 — market looks high). **Within the 6.5s, edge data says prefer Oklahoma State/SDSU over Fresno/Texas State.**
- **Reassigned P4 wrinkle:** Oklahoma State (TARP +10.0, SP+ #38) and Arkansas (SP+ #47) grade out well by SP+/TARP despite modest market totals — possible value if they slip in the pool because drafters misread them as their weak *real-conference* selves.
- **Bottom tier (Purdue 3.5, Colorado State 3.5):** avoid as anchors; these are the forced-replacement lingerers. Colorado State's new-HC/OC/QB reset is a hard under.

**Scarcity note:** this bucket has a thin usable top — really only Boise clears a comfortable anchor bar, with a cluster of contested 6.5s below. Per the depth-countdown, if you need Pac12 and Boise + the credible 6.5s (Oklahoma State, SDSU) start going, the drop-off to the 4.5/3.5 lingerers is steep. Spend a pick here **before** it thins to those.
